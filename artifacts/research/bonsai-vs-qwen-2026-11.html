Bonsai is the better agent. Qwen is the quicker everyday model.
Keep Qwen as the default; offer Bonsai for complex agent and coding work.

All numbers come from the independent deterministic judge over 59 cases (34 development, 25 held-out), run in isolated backends on private model servers. Matched arm: 2 repetitions per case. Best-practical arm: 1 repetition, configuration frozen on development cases first.

Echo Companion

Both models stay observer-only and give way at once to foreground work. Qwen's spoken wording passed the guard 15/15 at the 4-second budget, Bonsai's 11/15 (it drops the agent name "Echo" on Echo's own events); Bonsai answers faster (1.1 vs 1.7 s).

Recommendation

Optional alternative for complex tasks, not a replacement yet. Promote Bonsai after: a managed llama-server launcher with the tested flags and a VRAM check, owner live acceptance on real projects, and the memory-consent decision in #239. The default production model was not changed.
