Echo isn't only a reply.
It tells you what needs you, and connects your apps in one click.

Evidence: source changes with deterministic tests, a browser preview on the owner's PC with a throwaway data folder, and a live OAuth probe of every new hosted app. Signing in to an app and receiving a real check-in are the owner's checks in the installed app.

The old heartbeat never started from the app: it required two settings nothing sets. It now starts, skips quiet hours, never interrupts a conversation, hides its own prompt and leaves no trace when it has nothing to say.

See what you approve

Connect shows the steps, cautions and any field it needs. Your click is the approval, the same path as an agent's suggestion.

Sign in where it lives

Hosted apps open their own sign-in page; tokens stay encrypted on this PC. Disconnect signs out and forgets the app.

Before: 9 of 10 account cards said sign-in “isn't available yet”, and Notion, Linear and Jira/Confluence were marked unsupported although EchoSpeak could already sign in. All three connect now.
