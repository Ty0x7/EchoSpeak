What changed

Reminders

  • Plain words: "in 20 minutes", "Sunday at 4", "every weekday at 8".
  • One-time or repeating; ask Echo to list or cancel them, or cancel on the Routines page.
  • A reminder can also do a task when it fires; anything needing approval is skipped and reported.
  • Missed while EchoSpeak was closed? It arrives at the next start, marked late.

Notifications

  • Windows notifications for reminders, shown even when the window is closed to the tray.
  • Discord and Telegram receive them too, when set up.
  • The open chat refreshes when a reminder lands in it.

Keep running in the tray

  • Settings › General, off by default.
  • Closing hides EchoSpeak to the tray (Show hidden icons) instead of quitting.
  • Click the icon to reopen; right-click › Quit EchoSpeak to quit.

Safety

  • Only you can set reminders, and a routine can't schedule more.
  • A reminder with a task, set after reading web or app content, asks you first.
  • Stop everything holds reminders until you resume.

Verification and limits

Backend and web suites pass. The desktop shell compiles, with tests for its notification check. A scheduler pass proved a plain reminder posts into its chat with no model call and notifies exactly once. A real tray icon and real Windows notifications need the installed app, so that's owner testing. Reminders need EchoSpeak running (the tray keeps it running when you close the window, but not through a restart), can be up to a minute late, and clicking a notification doesn't open its chat.
