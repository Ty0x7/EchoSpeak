What changed

  • Correct the shared OpenAI-compatible stream parser used by Gemini, OpenAI and Grok while preserving indexed, fragmented streams.
  • Reject ambiguous argument fragments instead of assigning them to an unknown tool.
  • Keep the minimum supported thinking level for Gemini 2.5 Pro when Think is off, including background/review requests.
  • Strengthen Settings › Models › Test response for Gemini, OpenAI, Claude and Grok: check two harmless test calls, return their synthetic results and require a final response.
  • Keep catalog access separate from a working chat/tool check.
