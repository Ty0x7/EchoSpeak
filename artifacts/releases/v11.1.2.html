What changed

What's new

  • A slimmer sidebar. The left sidebar is about a third narrower, so the chat gets more room.
  • The "Navigation" bar at the top is gone.
  • Group chats, Projects and Artifacts show by default.
  • Blue in the light theme. Page icons in the sidebar are blue, and text across the app is blue too: the sidebar, pages, Settings and voice.
  • A livelier Echo in voice.
  • The circles behind Echo are gone, and so is the white outline.
  • Echo reacts to what is happening.
  • If you turn on reduced motion in Windows, Echo stays still.
  • Echo in the titlebar has a soft glow behind him, so the white face stands out on the white title bar.
