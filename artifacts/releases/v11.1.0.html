What changed

Light and dark

  • Light theme, the new default. White surfaces, navy text, light blue edges and a blue accent:
  • blue gradient buttons for the main actions;
  • white message cards with a blue edge;
  • small uppercase section labels;
  • outlined tabs in the right panel.
  • Echo glows. In light mode Echo has a blue outer ring, and in voice mode a large blue glow behind him.
  • Jarvis and Glados are blue in light mode and black in dark mode.
  • Dark theme unchanged. The black theme looks as it always has.
  • Choose your look in Settings › General › Appearance, or on the last step of setup:
  • Light
  • Dark
  • Match system, which follows Windows.
  • Code blocks, errors and warnings use colours that read well on white.

Setup

  • Setup opens in its own window.
  • A slimmer header. The title, steps and Set up later now sit in one row.
Setup and compatibility
Same as 11.0.0. No settings or data migration. Your theme choice is stored on this device.
