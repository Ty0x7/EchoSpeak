# Conversation audit, 16 November 2026: findings, causes, fixes and tests

> The owner's real Echo conversations after the 11.14.0 identity update (7 conversations, 65 messages,
> 33 execution records, 49 tool records, about 13,400 journal events), read in full by Codex on
> 16 November 2026 from read-only SQLite backups. This page turns that audit into engineering
> records. Each failure here has a root cause found in source, a fix, and a regression test in
> `apps/backend/tests/test_agent_first.py` (unit level) or a live case in
> `agent-first-live.py` (real model). Architecture and results are in [agent-first.md](agent-first.md).
>
> The owner's conversations are not copied into the repository. Quotes are short and only where the wording
> matters.

## What was deliberately not changed

- **Permission enforcement.** Switches, toolsets, the policy check, approvals, Private/Offline mode and the
  Chat call guard are untouched. Every change above is on the describing side, or makes the record more honest.
- **The selected model.** Nothing switches models; profiles only change instructions and tools.
- **Legacy `environments` mode.** Behaves as before (`test_legacy_code_mode_is_unchanged`).
- **"Runtime completion does not establish answer correctness"** (finding 31) is still true in general. The work
  record now catches the case where a deliverable plainly doesn't exist. It does not judge whether an answer is right.
