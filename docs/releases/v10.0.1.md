# EchoSpeak 10.0.1

Answers you can see, not just read, plus a sidebar that always shows what matters.

## Highlights

- **Visual answers.** When the data fits, answers come with a card. Every card is built from real data. The agent never types in the prices, links or numbers itself.
  - **Weather:** today plus a 7-day forecast.
  - **Stocks:** a price chart, e.g. "compare Nvidia and AMD this year".
  - **Shopping:** product cards with real prices and store links, marked "as of" a time.
  - **Videos and pictures:** video cards with durations, and an image gallery with a lightbox.
  - **Sports:** score cards.
  - **Sources:** numbered chips at the end of any answer that searched the web.
- **Artifacts.**
  - Ask for something to use, like "build me a tip calculator", and it opens in a side panel.
  - Ask for a change and you get version 2. You can flip between versions and restore an old one.
  - From the panel you can copy, download, open in your browser, or go full screen.
  - Apps run sealed off: no internet, and no access to your files or EchoSpeak.
  - Everything your agents make is listed on the new **Artifacts** page.
- **Richer replies.**
  - Highlighted code with copy and file names.
  - Tables you can sort and copy.
  - Diagrams (for example, how a TCP handshake works).
  - Math formulas, timelines and step lists, side-by-side comparisons, key-number tiles, and maps.
- **Sports that just work.** Scores, schedules, results and standings now come from ESPN.
  - No API key needed.
  - Just name the team ("Nuggets score?") or the league.
- **New sidebar.**
  - **New chat**, **Search chats** and the pages (**Group chats**, **Projects**, **Artifacts**, **Routines**) stay pinned at the top.
  - **Agents**, **Chats** and **Projects** fill the rest, with two dividers you can drag. Double-click a divider to reset it. Sizes are remembered.
  - Agent rows are compact: hover one to see details.
- **Friendlier Echo.**
  - Echo is warmer and more helpful, and gives complete answers instead of short, sassy ones.
  - If you never edited Echo's personality, your copy updates automatically. If you did, it's left alone.
- **New look.** The Echo face is now the app icon, in the taskbar and the title bar.

## Smaller changes

- Links in answers open in your normal browser.
- While an agent is answering, its reply no longer runs into the Stop/timer pill.
- Long chat and project lists scroll inside their section instead of squashing together.
- Pictures from the web load through EchoSpeak with size and time limits, and only from public addresses.

## Requirements

Same as 10.0.0. No new settings.
