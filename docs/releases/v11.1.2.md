# EchoSpeak 11.1.2

A design update for the light theme: a slimmer sidebar, blue text and icons, and a livelier Echo in voice conversations. Everything from 11.1.1 carries forward.

## What's new

- **A slimmer sidebar.** The left sidebar is about a third narrower, so the chat gets more room.
  - The "Navigation" bar at the top is gone. Collapse the sidebar with the small arrow next to **New chat**.
  - Group chats, Projects and Artifacts show by default. Choose **More** to show Routines, Creations and Learning, and **Less** to hide them again. EchoSpeak remembers your choice.
- **Blue in the light theme.** Page icons in the sidebar are blue, and text across the app is blue too: the sidebar, pages, Settings and voice. Messages in the chat keep a neutral color so long answers stay easy to read.
- **A livelier Echo in voice.**
  - The circles behind Echo are gone, and so is the white outline. A glow in Echo's own shape sits behind him instead.
  - Echo reacts to what is happening. He leans in and his glow follows your voice while he listens. He looks up while he thinks, scans while he works, bounces while he speaks, and gives a happy hop when he finishes. If something goes wrong, his glow turns red.
  - If you turn on reduced motion in Windows, Echo stays still.
- **Echo in the titlebar** has a soft glow behind him, so the white face stands out on the white title bar.

The dark theme keeps its look, with the same slimmer sidebar and the new voice animations in white.

## Requirements

Same as 11.1.1. No data migration.

## Validation

- Checked in the light and dark themes: the sidebar (open, collapsed, More and Less), pages, Settings, and each of Echo's voice states.
- Frontend type checks, 116 unit tests and the production build.
